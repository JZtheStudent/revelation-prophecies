## I. The True Nature of the Reformation: Reform, Not Separation from Babylon

The Protestant Reformation initiated by Martin Luther, together with all denominations derived from it, corrected numerous superficial abuses within Roman Catholicism in history — including the sale of indulgences, erroneous sacramental practices, extreme centralization of papal authority, and systemic ecclesiastical corruption. ^PFP-00227

From a human perspective, the Reformation appears to be a movement restoring biblical truth and pure faith. ^PFP-00228

Nevertheless, judged by the complete prophetic framework of Revelation and the definition of Babylon the Great: Protestantism never departed from the two fatal foundational apostasies that define the harlot system. It achieved only internal reform, never breaking away from Babylon. ^PFP-00229

These two foundational errors are the ultimate reasons for Babylon’s divine judgment: ^PFP-00230

- The man‑made doctrine of the Trinity, which distorts the original biblical revelation concerning the relationship between the Father and the Son;
- The abolition of the God‑ordained seventh‑day Sabbath and the alteration of God’s appointed times and laws, establishing Sunday worship in its place.

^PFP-00231

Without exception, all Protestant denominations fully inherit and uphold these two core apostate doctrines of Roman Catholicism. The Reformation only adjusted external rituals, church governance, and minor doctrinal errors within the Babylon system, yet never challenged or abandoned its fatal spiritual foundations. ^PFP-00232

This perfectly fulfils Revelation 17:5, which names Rome “the mother of harlots and abominations of the earth”: ^PFP-00233

- Roman Catholicism is the mother harlot, the original source of the apostate system;
- All Protestant denominations split, reformed, and derived from Catholicism are the daughter harlots, branches dependent on the mother system.

^PFP-00234

Therefore, the Reformation was not a rebirth of faith nor a restoration of pure truth. It was merely internal division and partial reform within the Roman apostate system of Babylon. ^PFP-00235

^PFP-00239

Final Verdict: Protestantism is not a system of pure biblical truth. It is a modified, lightweight version of apostate Babylon, still essentially a branch of the harlot system. ^PFP-00240
