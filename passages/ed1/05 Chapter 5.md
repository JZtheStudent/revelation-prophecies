# Chapter 5 — Babylon the Great: Her Threefold Identity and Final Judgment

(These are independent interpretive reflections and do not represent the consensus of mainstream churches. Interpretive sources include the Book of Daniel and Revelation 17–18, together with Hebrew Old Testament texts. This chapter follows the chronological framework of the seven heads and ten horns and the Roman beast system established in Chapter 5. It defines the threefold, unified nature of Babylon the Great, unfolds the order of judgment by the ten horns, and the symbolism of her garments, closing the prophetic timeline consistently with the preceding material.) ^PFP-00204

## I. The Threefold Definition of Babylon the Great, the Harlot

Babylon the Great, the harlot in Scripture, is not a single individual. She is a threefold, united, end‑time apostate power, sharing one origin. All three dimensions coexist and are judged together by the ten horns: ^PFP-00205

Spiritual dimension: the apostate religious system This is the foundational and fatal core of the harlot’s sin: corrupting the truth of Scripture by inventing the doctrine of the Trinity. This man‑made doctrine departs from the original biblical revelation concerning the Father, the Son and the Holy Spirit. Beyond that, it alters the divinely ordained appointed times, replacing the Sabbath with Sunday, setting church tradition above God’s explicit Sabbath command. This fulfils the prophecy in Daniel that “he shall think to change the times and the law”. From this root arise further human teachings: papal authority, Mary as mediatrix, intercession of saints, the doctrine of purgatory, and more, elevating human tradition above the plain written word of God. ^PFP-00206

This is spiritual fornication: a religious system that ought to be loyal only to God invents doctrines, changes God’s law and appointed times, enters covenant with earthly political powers, and deceives the nations with false teaching, drawing the inhabitants of the earth into spiritual adultery. She is called “the mother of harlots and abominations of the earth” (Rev. 17:5), signifying that she gives birth to and nurtures all apostate religion and false worship throughout the ages. ^PFP-00207

Political‑institutional dimension: the authority structure of the Roman Curia The harlot is not merely a deviation in doctrine. She is a globally organised power with centralised governance, ruling authority, diplomatic standing and treaty‑making capacity: the Vatican ecclesiastical‑political system. ^PFP-00208

Scripture describes her marks clearly: she sits upon many waters (Rev. 17:1), having dominion over peoples, multitudes, nations and tongues; she commits fornication with the kings of the earth (Rev. 18:3), signing concordats, forming political alliances and binding herself closely with governments across the world. She is rich and luxurious, carrying out cross‑border commerce and spiritual trafficking. ^PFP-00209

This is the very basis for our selection of the ten horn nations: the ten horns are Catholic sovereign states within the Roman tradition that have covenanted with and sustained the harlot’s power. ^PFP-00210

Geographical dimension: the great city of Rome Revelation 17 focuses on the woman as a symbolic power; Revelation 18 turns to the city as a physical entity. Scripture states plainly that Babylon the Great is the great city which reigns over the kings of the earth (Rev. 17:18). Geographically, this city is Rome in Italy, the headquarters, power base and central dwelling of the harlot’s whole operation. ^PFP-00211

## II. The Relationship between the Three Dimensions

- The apostate religious system forms her foundation of deception, marked by the invented Trinity doctrine and the alteration of the Sabbath;
- The curial political structure is the vessel through which she exercises influence, united with earthly rulers by concordats;
- The city of Rome is the headquarters and territorial root of this power system.

^PFP-00212

The three belong to one unified power of Babylon the Great. Doctrine sustains the institutional system; the system unites itself with nations by covenants; and the whole power is rooted in the city of Rome. Therefore the judgment is one final judgment, falling upon all three layers simultaneously. ^PFP-00213

## III. The Judgment Sequence: the Ten Horns Destroy the Harlot (Rev. 17:16–17, Chapter 18)

The text states clearly: the ten horns are ten kings upon the beast. It is the ten horns themselves who hate and destroy the harlot. The beast is only the vessel bearing the ten horns and does not join in destroying her. The whole sequence is foreordained by God: ^PFP-00214

Divine predestination and stirring “For God hath put in their hearts to fulfil his will, and to agree, and give their kingdom unto the beast, until the words of God shall be fulfilled.” (Rev. 17:17) ^PFP-00215

At the close of the end time, God stirs the ten horn nations we have identified. These states, which once made concordats with the Vatican and sustained the harlot’s power for a long season, will reverse their alignment under God’s appointment and turn against her. ^PFP-00216

The betrayal and extermination by the ten horns (Rev. 17:16) “And the ten horns which thou sawest upon the beast, these shall hate the whore, and shall make her desolate and naked, and shall eat her flesh, and burn her with fire.” This verse describes a threefold destruction corresponding to the threefold identity of the harlot: ① Hate her: revoke all concordats with the Vatican, sever diplomatic ties, end all official cooperation and dismantle her political power; ② Make her desolate and naked: expose the false doctrinal system, lay bare its apostasy of inventing the Trinity and altering the Sabbath, leaving the religious system stripped of all cover and refuge; ③ Eat her flesh and burn her with fire: judgment falls upon her headquarters, the city of Rome. Geographical Babylon is burned and overthrown, fully fulfilling the cry of Revelation 18: “Babylon the great is fallen, is fallen!” ^PFP-00217

## IV. The Final Outcome of Judgment in Revelation 18

- Religiously: the apostate Catholic system marked by the invented Trinity and altered Sabbath collapses completely and deceives the nations no more;
- Politically: the global governing authority of the Vatican and the network of concordats binding it to nations is dissolved;
- Geographically: the city of Rome becomes desolate, a habitation of devils and every unclean spirit, to rise no more.

^PFP-00218

## V. Symbolism of the Harlot’s Garments

The harlot is clothed in purple and scarlet, decked with gold, precious stones and pearls (Rev. 17:4). These colours and ornaments are not mere decorative description. They carry defined prophetic symbolism, aligned with the liturgy and spiritual corruption of the curial system: ^PFP-00219

Purple: in the ancient Roman Empire, purple was reserved for emperors and the elite, symbolising kingship and supreme status. This prefigures the religious system’s craving for earthly royal honour, seeking equal standing with the kings of the earth and dominion over many nations. ^PFP-00220

Scarlet: in Scripture, scarlet often symbolises sin and bloodshed. It points to the spiritual defilement of this system and the blood of righteous saints shed across generations because of its teaching and persecution. The text later says she is drunken with the blood of the saints and of the witnesses of Yeshua (Rev. 17:6). ^PFP-00221

Gold, precious stones and pearls: symbols of luxury, wealth and commercial gain, corresponding to Revelation 18, where she amasses vast riches through alliances and spiritual trafficking, drawing the world with material splendour. ^PFP-00222

Historically, the purple and scarlet vestments of bishops and popes are no accident. This liturgical colour scheme inherits the symbolic language of Roman elite power, an outward expression of the same power structure. Yet the order of priority must be kept: the prophecy first uses these colours symbolically; the apostate system later adopts them for its own robes to display worldly authority. The text does not borrow papal vestments to form the prophecy. ^PFP-00223

The colours themselves are not unclean. Their symbolism points to two evils within the system: craving earthly royal power and being stained with the blood of saints. The colours are only markers. The true corruption lies in her false doctrines, her persecution of saints and her spiritual adultery through covenants with earthly kings. ^PFP-00224

## VI. Summary

- Babylon the Great the harlot is a threefold end‑time power: an apostate religious system, the political authority of the curia, and the physical city of Rome;
- The two core marks of this apostate system are the invented doctrine of the Trinity and the alteration of God’s ordained Sabbath;
- The ten horns are the Catholic nations of the Roman tradition that once covenanted with and sustained the harlot. They rest upon the beast and are stirred by God to execute judgment upon her;
- Chapter 17 reveals the nature of the harlot and the cause of judgment; Chapter 18 describes the fall of the city and the final end of her power.
