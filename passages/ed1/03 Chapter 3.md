# Chapter 3 — The Fulfillment of the First to the Sixth Seals

> [!scripture]
> Revelation 6:1–2: When the Lamb opened the first seal, I heard one of the four living creatures say with a voice like thunder, “Come!” I looked, and there was a white horse. Its rider held a bow, and a crown was given to him. He went out conquering and to conquer.

^PFP-00135

We may examine the prophecy of the white‑horse rider of the first seal by comparing it with other biblical descriptions of the Messiah. The white horse itself is a symbol that mimics the Messiah. In Revelation 19, the Messiah also rides a white horse, yet a sharp two‑edged sword proceeds out of His mouth, and He will rule the nations with a rod of iron. By sharp contrast, the rider of the first seal carries only a bow, with no sword or iron rod. This is the mark that he is an impostor. ^PFP-00136

First John makes clear that the Antichrist was not reserved for the distant end time; many antichrists had already arisen in the earliest apostolic era. The essence of Antichrist is a pseudo‑Christian system that counterfeits the Messiah. The false‑apostle line represented by Paul constitutes the earliest manifestation of Antichrist. Expanding under a seemingly holy outward guise and imitating the pattern of the Messiah, it receives a crown and goes out conquering and to conquer. It penetrates steadily within the Roman system and gradually supplants the pure faith transmitted by the original apostles. This spiritual victory of deception marks the beginning of the successive judgments and disasters symbolized by the red, black and pale horses. The boundaries adopted herein are defined according to the Roman system of the seven heads and ten horns in Revelation, and do not follow the standard statistical frameworks of modern geography or historiography. ^PFP-00137

> [!scripture]
> Revelation 6:3–4: When he opened the second seal, I heard the second living creature say, “Come!” And another horse came out, fiery red. Authority was given to its rider to take peace away from the earth, so that people would slay one another, and a great sword was given to him.

^PFP-00138

The second seal is a general prophetic statement and does not correspond to one single specific historical event. From the time Revelation was written onward, warfare persisted continuously across continental Europe within the bounds of the Roman system, repeatedly taking peace from the earth and setting people against one another. This is a long‑term fulfillment of judgment following the expansion of the false teaching of the first seal. ^PFP-00139

> [!scripture]
> Revelation 6:5–6: When the Lamb opened the third seal, I heard the third living creature say, “Come!” I looked, and there was a black horse. Its rider held a balance in his hand. And I heard a voice in the midst of the four living creatures say, “A denarius for a quart of wheat, and a denarius for three quarts of barley; but do not harm the oil and the wine.”

^PFP-00140

We may examine the fulfillment of the third‑seal prophecy through the climatic disruption, famine and pestilence that struck continental Europe within the Roman system starting in 536 CE. The supervolcanic eruption of 536 CE triggered a volcanic winter. Summer temperatures across the Northern Hemisphere plummeted, sunlight grew dim, grain harvests failed for successive years, staple food prices surged, and severe famine broke out. Then in 541 CE, the Justinian Plague spread widely throughout the Mediterranean and the Roman sphere. The defining features of the text are these: basic grain staples (wheat and barley) become exorbitantly expensive, a day’s wage barely sustains one person, yet oil and wine remain unharmed. This distinction matches the historical reality: grains are annual crops directly vulnerable to harvest failure caused by climatic shock, while olive and grape vines, perennial woody crops, endured the short‑term cold disaster with far less disruption. ^PFP-00141

Other calamities within the history of the Roman system include the Antonine Plague, the Cyprian Plague, the Black Death and numerous local famines. Nevertheless, no other event satisfies the full set of criteria simultaneously: widespread multi‑year volcanic cooling, region‑wide grain failure driving up grain prices, preservation of oil and wine supplies from major collapse, and a subsequent large‑scale pandemic. Therefore, the disaster period of 536–544 CE is the only suitable historical fulfillment of this prophecy. References: McCormick (2018); Procopius, History of the Wars; Harper, The Fate of Rome (2017). ^PFP-00142

> [!scripture]
> Revelation 6:7–8: When the Lamb opened the fourth seal, I heard the fourth living creature say, “Come!” I looked, and there was a pale horse. The name of its rider was Death, and Hades followed close behind him. Authority was given to them over a quarter of the earth, to kill with sword, famine, pestilence and wild beasts.

^PFP-00143

We examine the fulfillment of the fourth‑seal prophecy by reviewing the death toll among combatant nations on continental Europe within the Roman system during the Second World War. Scope of statistics: excluding Britain, the Soviet Union/Russia, and neutral states such as Switzerland and Spain. All war‑related excess deaths are included: battlefield fatalities, executions, deaths in concentration camps (including the widely recognised figure of approximately six million European Jews), air‑raid casualties, deaths from abuse of prisoners of war, deaths from siege‑related famine, disease, and mortality caused by displacement. The total ranges from 38 million to 42 million. The pre‑war population of this region in 1939 stood at roughly 185 million, so war‑related excess deaths accounted for 21%–22% of the pre‑war population. The gap between this figure and the prophetic quarter (25%) may be attributed to statistical error arising from incomplete source records and baseline census limitations, which aligns with the prophecy of the fourth seal. ^PFP-00144

The regional boundary follows the Roman system of the seven heads and ten horns in Revelation, and is not the standard scope used in modern geography or historiography. The classic statistic of six million Jewish victims is calculated within this same defined region. References: Overmans (2000); Institute of National Remembrance (IPN, Poland, 2009); Milward (1979); Frumkin (1951). ^PFP-00145

> [!scripture]
> Revelation 6:9–11: When he opened the fifth seal, I saw under the altar the souls of those who had been slain for the word of God and for the testimony they had borne. They cried out with a loud voice, “O Sovereign Lord, holy and true, how long before you will judge and avenge our blood on those who dwell on the earth?” Then they were each given a white robe and told to rest a little longer, until the number of their fellow servants and their brothers should be complete, who were to be killed as they themselves had been.

^PFP-00146

The fifth seal differs markedly in nature from the preceding four seals. While the first four unfold progressively across the history of the Roman system and can be examined against historical events, the fifth seal offers no external historical markers that we can identify and verify. This passage records the cry of the souls of all martyrs slain for the true faith throughout all generations, pleading before God for righteous judgment. God gives them white robes as the token of their righteousness and bids them wait patiently until the full number of martyrs is completed. This is a spiritual promise of justice and waiting, with no corresponding historical event we can pinpoint. Therefore, we do not assign it to any specific historical fulfillment. ^PFP-00147

> [!scripture]
> Revelation 6:12–17: When he opened the sixth seal, I looked, and behold, there was a great earthquake, and the sun became black as sackcloth, and the full moon became like blood. The stars of the sky fell to the earth as the fig tree sheds its winter fruit when shaken by a gale. The sky vanished like a scroll that is being rolled up, and every mountain and island was removed from its place. Then the kings of the earth and the great ones and the generals and the rich and the powerful, and everyone, slave and free, hid themselves in the caves and among the rocks of the mountains, calling to the mountains and rocks, “Fall on us and hide us from the face of him who is seated on the throne, and from the wrath of the Lamb, for the great day of their wrath has come, and who can stand?”

^PFP-00148

The sixth seal likewise does not belong to events fulfilled incrementally throughout history. It is a prophetic vision of the Messiah’s return and the great day of God’s wrath, a preview of the final judgment. This describes events that will take place in the future, when the Lamb appears in judgment, the whole created order is shaken, and every earthly authority flees before Him. It must not be conflated with any earthquake or celestial sign recorded in human history. ^PFP-00149
