# Chapter 4 — Two Beasts and the Image of the Beast

(These are independent interpretive reflections and do not represent the consensus of mainstream churches. Interpretive sources include the Gospel of Matthew, the Gospel of John, the Book of Malachi, the Book of Revelation, and Hebrew Old Testament texts. This chapter links the Lateran Treaty, the 1933 Reichskonkordat, Axis relations, the persecution of Jehovah’s Witnesses, the definition of the image of the beast, grammatical analysis of the mark, and the full logical positioning of the roles. It also includes textual-critical notes on Revelation 13:15 from ancient Aramaic manuscripts.) ^PFP-00150

## I. Revelation 17: The Core Framework of the Seven Heads and Ten Horns

### 1. Foundational Principle

The beast in Revelation 17 is the fourth beast in Daniel — the Roman system. The text clearly distinguishes the seven heads from the ten horns, and they must not be confused: ^PFP-00151

Seven Heads: Seven successive royal forms of the same Roman beast rising and falling in historical sequence, forming the main chronological line. ^PFP-00152

Ten Horns: Ten authorities coexisting in the end time, developing in parallel; they do not replace or succeed the main line of the seven heads. ^PFP-00153

Summary: The seven heads are successive ruling authorities along the main historical line; the ten horns are parallel national powers in the end time. Their symbolic identities, timelines, and prophetic missions are entirely separate. ^PFP-00154

### 2. Fixed Chronological Order of the Seven Heads (the sole main thread of the whole prophecy)

First Head: the Unified Roman Empire (27 BCE – 395 CE) The undivided original form of the beast, the starting point of the entire lineage of Roman succession. ^PFP-00155

Second Head: the Eastern Roman (Byzantine Empire, 395–1453) The longest-surviving legitimate successor to Roman authority after the division of the empire. ^PFP-00156

Third Head: the Western Roman Empire (395–476) It coexisted briefly with the Eastern Roman but fell earlier. Within this prophetic symbolic framework, it counts as an independent “head”, representing a separate historical stage after the imperial division. Note: The Eastern and Western Roman Empires overlapped in time, yet the prophecy treats them as two separate royal stages. The division of the empire itself is not counted as an additional head. ^PFP-00157

Fourth Head: the Holy Roman Empire (800–1806) The inheritor of secular Roman tradition in Western Europe, crowned by the Pope and dominated by the House of Habsburg. It served as the central secular ally of the Catholic papacy in the Middle Ages, forming a covenantal church‑state partnership and becoming the model secular power supporting papal authority. ^PFP-00158

Fifth Head: the Papal States / Vatican Church‑State System (538–1798; final loss of secular territory in 1870) Two landmark events occurred simultaneously in 538 CE: ^PFP-00159

- Belisarius eliminated the last Arian kingdoms, removing secular checks on papal power;
- The Third Council of Orléans issued decrees abolishing the seventh‑day Sabbath and imposing Sunday worship by law, altering God’s commandments through official religious statute.

^PFP-00160

Core marker: Religious authority usurping divine sovereignty to exercise legitimate dominion over nations. ^PFP-00161

In 1798, Napoleon captured the Pope, inflicting a deadly wound on the Papal States; ^PFP-00162

The span from 538 to 1798 is exactly 1260 years. ^PFP-00163

The prophecy of “forty‑two months” follows the historicist day‑for‑year principle: 42 months = 1260 years. ^PFP-00164

Interpretive note: This 1260‑year period is the full ordained term of the granted authority, not a period of constant peak power. The objection that “538 was merely an early formative stage and not yet exercising unrestrained dominion” can be answered by the nature of prophetic language: Scripture records the complete authorized time span, not a curve of fluctuating power intensity. From 538 onward, this church‑state authority possessed the legal mandate described in the prophecy. It was weak and constrained by external forces in its early centuries, then expanded to its peak, and declined toward the end. Yet the core authority remained unbroken along a continuous timeline until the fatal wound in 1798, marking the close of the 1260‑year prophetic cycle. Chronological logic: The period of unrestrained dominion falls strictly before the head receives the deadly wound; the expiry of that authorized period coincides with the moment of wounding. ^PFP-00165

Sixth Head: the Austro‑Hungarian Empire (1867–1918) The final legitimate continuation of the main Habsburg line, inheriting the Catholic Roman tradition of continental Europe and serving as the last major secular protector of the papacy on the European mainland. ^PFP-00166

Seventh Head: Mussolini’s Italian Fascist Regime (1922–1943) It fully fulfils the text “it must continue a short space”, the shortest‑lived and final successive regime on the seven‑head main line. ^PFP-00167

### 3. The Eighth Beast (Rev. 17:11)

The beast itself is the eighth, proceeding out of the seven heads and destined for destruction. The eighth is not a new regime; it is the revived form of the wounded Fifth Head (the Vatican), corresponding to the Lateran Treaty of 1929, which restored Vatican sovereignty and reactivated its church‑state authority. ^PFP-00168

- Spanish Empire: a colonial maritime hegemony, grouped among the parallel ten horns, not a successive hegemon on the main line.

^PFP-00169

All prophetic conditions were fully established within this window: ^PFP-00170

- The Sixth Head, the Austro‑Hungarian Empire, was reigning;
- The Seventh Head, Italian Fascism, had not yet arisen;

^PFP-00171

### 7. Grammatical Exegesis of Revelation 17:12

- “They shall receive authority, as kings, with the beast”: prophetic present combined with future tense, denoting the short, united reign predestined by God for the end time.

^PFP-00179

## II. Revelation 13: Historical Fulfilment Built upon the Chronological Framework of Chapter 17

### 1. The Unifying Principle of the Beast and its Heads (Distinguish the Visionary Image from the Angel’s Chronological Interpretation)

- What John sees in chapter 13 is a static image: a single beast with seven heads and ten horns, all shown side by side;
- Yet the angel’s authoritative explanation in chapter 17 clarifies that the seven heads do not rule simultaneously; they are seven kings (Rev. 17:10), rising one after another, one falling and another arising, representing the beast’s manifestation in successive historical stages.

^PFP-00181

Core conclusion: The beast does not exist independently apart from these successive kings. The beast’s authority is revealed through these successive regimes. There is no abstract mother beast separate from all the heads; whichever head is ruling in a given era is the beast manifested in that period. ^PFP-00182

Applied to Rev. 13:3: the one that receives the deadly wound and is healed is the Fifth Head, the Papal States. Since this head is the beast’s manifestation in that era, the text simply calls it the beast. This resolves a common interpretive tension: if one mistakenly assumes the seven heads coexist simultaneously, the beast is taken as a composite whole containing all seven heads. Following the angel’s explanation in chapter 17 and recognizing the heads as successive rulers, the single reigning head in any period is the beast for that season. ^PFP-00183

Final conclusion: In prophetic identity, the statement that “one of the heads was wounded to death and healed” is equivalent to the whole beast being wounded to death and healed. ^PFP-00184

### 2. The First Beast out of the Sea: Precise Fulfilment in the Fifth Head, the Vatican Church‑State System

- 1798: The secular regime of the Papal States was overthrown, and the Fifth Head received the deadly wound;
- 1929: The Lateran Treaty was signed, restoring Vatican sovereignty and international church‑state authority, and the deadly wound was fully healed.

^PFP-00185

It follows that the sea beast of Revelation 13, wounded to death, healed and worshipped by all the earth, is the restored Vatican church‑state system. ^PFP-00186

### 3. The Role of the Second Beast out of the Earth

The beast out of the earth does not belong to the seven‑head royal main line. It is a separate secular supporting power raised up apart from that line. Its three exclusive missions: ^PFP-00187

- It exercises the authority of the first beast before it;
- It deceives the inhabitants of the earth, compelling them to worship the first sea beast whose deadly wound was healed;
- It makes an image of the first beast, reproducing the coercive church‑state system.

^PFP-00188

### 4. Correct Definition of the Image of the Beast and the Mark of the Beast (Not a Physical Statue, but an Institutional Replica)

#### (1) The Image of the Beast: Replica of the Church‑State Covenant System

The archetypal pattern of the first beast comes from its golden era before the wound: the Papal States held secular royal territory, paired with the Holy Roman Empire as its secular arm. The two were bound by covenant, with religious authority and secular state power tightly integrated to maintain a church‑state order. The Holy Roman Empire (the Fourth Head) was the model secular power supporting the papacy. ^PFP-00189

The essence of the image of the beast: within its own territory, the beast out of the earth reproduces this ancient covenant pattern. A secular state enters a church‑state concordat with the healed first beast of the Vatican. The secular power assumes the role once held by the Holy Roman Empire, using state power to uphold papal religious authority and rebuild a bonded church‑state order that controls faith and punishes dissenters. ^PFP-00190

#### (2) The Mark of the Beast: Internal Assent and Practical Compliance

A clear distinction must be maintained: ^PFP-00191

- The image of the beast = the church‑state ruling system itself;
- The mark of the beast ≠ a physical tattoo on the skin.

^PFP-00192

- Mark on the forehead: inward intellectual assent to the doctrines and order of the beast and its image;
- Mark on the hand: practical compliance with the rules of this system in livelihood and economic activity.

^PFP-00193

The system enforces coercion through economic controls: those who refuse the mark cannot buy or sell. This forces everyone to choose: either submit to the order of the beast, or face economic blockade and deprivation. ^PFP-00194

#### (3) Exegesis of the Number 666

The text states “Here is wisdom. Let him that hath understanding count the number of the beast”, which itself indicates the clue is hidden, not obvious at first glance. If the title were openly printed on current official titles, ordinary people could identify it easily, and no special spiritual wisdom would be needed to discover and calculate it. This title is not among the Vatican’s current official styles; it survives in medieval forged documents such as the Donation of Constantine and is repeatedly cited in the theological writings of bishops through the centuries. Its hidden character fits the design of the prophecy. ^PFP-00195

Scripture identifies 666 as the number of a man, pointing to this human‑constructed system that exalts human authority and rebels against God’s sovereignty. In the historicist tradition, this number corresponds to the element within the first beast system that “speaks great blasphemous words”: papal authority. ^PFP-00196

Title background: The Vatican’s current official title is Vicarius Christi (Vicar of Christ). Vicarius Filii Dei (Vicar of the Son of God) is not listed among its present official titles, yet the two share the same core meaning: the claim that the Pope exercises heavenly authority on earth in place of Messiah, a formulation found in medieval papal documents. ^PFP-00197

Roman numeral calculation (classical Latin U and V are interchangeable; letters with no numerical value are omitted): V(5)+I(1)+C(100)+I(1)+U(5)+I(1)+L(50)+I(1)+I(1)+D(500)+I(1) = 666. ^PFP-00198

Key distinction of ownership: The number 666 belongs exclusively to the first sea beast (the Vatican papal system), not the second beast out of the earth. The second beast only leads men to worship the first beast and makes its image; it does not carry the 666 prophetic marker itself. ^PFP-00199
