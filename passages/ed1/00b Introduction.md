# Introduction

This book is a sequel to Perfectly Fulfilled Prophecy, the Destiny of Mankind. In the preceding volume, I constructed a rigorous framework of biblical chronology, limiting the margin of error for the entire chronological sequence to no more than half a year, which serves as the foundation for this prophetic study. That work focused on approximately twenty numerical prophecies. Unlike descriptive prophecies, which often yield diverse interpretations due to divergent interpretive frameworks, numerical prophecies carry inherent rigidity and uniqueness, permitting only one valid fulfillment. The former volume examined most numerical prophecies in the Old Testament, alongside a small number from the New Testament, demonstrating that all of them have been fulfilled in one definite, univocal manner. While the earlier work surveyed the fulfillment of Old Testament and some New Testament prophecies, the chronological sequence and textual details of the Book of Revelation remained undeveloped in full. This volume takes Revelation as its primary thread, interfacing with the prophetic books of Daniel, Zechariah, Isaiah, Ezekiel and other Old Testament prophets, to unfold a coherent end‑times timeline within one unified prophetic framework. ^PFP-00013

This book contends that Revelation is not an isolated, brand‑new revelation. Instead, it builds upon the prophetic system already established by the Old Testament prophets. Every vision and every temporal proclamation within it align harmoniously with the revelations given beforehand. It will be argued herein that the forty‑two months in which the Gentiles trample the holy city, recorded in Revelation chapter 11; the one thousand two hundred and sixty days during which the woman flees into the wilderness in Revelation chapter 12 (corresponding to the years 688–1948 CE); the sequence of seal judgments up to the sixth seal; the beast from the sea and the beast from the earth; and the seven‑headed, ten‑horned governmental system have all found their fulfillment within history. Meanwhile, the judgment sequence of the seven trumpets and seven bowls corresponds to the final seven years within the seventy weeks of Daniel chapter 9. This final seven is not fulfilled as a single continuous span, but unfolds across two eras: the first segment is the earthly ministry of the Messiah two thousand years ago, and the second is the end‑times judgment period. Together, these two segments complete this final seven. ^PFP-00014

The consummation of all prophecy is the millennial kingdom ruled by the Messiah. This millennial hope is likewise not exclusive to Revelation; it is the promised witness shared by Isaiah, Ezekiel, Zechariah, Daniel and all the prophets. In this exegetical work, priority is given to ancient translations closer to the original text (HRV, the Lamsa Aramaic translation, and others). Additions introduced in later manuscripts and renderings are distinguished, and the internal consistency of Scripture itself stands as the foundation of interpretation. Differing from the popular fragmented exegetical methods in circulation, this work weaves the seals, trumpets, bowls, the image of the beast, the mark of the beast, and the battle of Armageddon into one continuous, self‑consistent timeline. ^PFP-00015

The reader will see that God’s prophetic word is unified from beginning to end, without two contradictory, disconnected plans. From the prophecies of Isaiah, Ezekiel and Daniel, to the vision John beheld on the isle of Patmos, onward to the Messiah’s return and the establishment of the millennial kingdom, there runs a single thread of redemption and judgment. The purpose of this book is to lay out this coherent prophetic timeline clearly, to manifest the precise fulfillment of biblical prophecy. This book covers the following major themes: ^PFP-00016

## I. Comparison of Core Doctrines: The Biblical Teaching of the Son of God, the Later Trinity, and the Revelation of the Qur’an

The Qur’an declares plainly that the Most High Allah has no son, no associate in sovereignty, nor any counsellor who shares His counsel. The original Arabic Kufic inscriptions lining the interior of the Dome of the Rock in Jerusalem, completed in 692 CE, constitute an ancient, authoritative documentary witness. These inscriptions were engraved expressly to refute the doctrine of the Trinity and the teaching that the Son is co‑equal with the Father, which was being propagated by the Catholic Church at that time. ^PFP-00017

Here we must establish a foundational dividing line for this entire exegetical project: the doctrine of the Trinity is not the original revelation of Scripture, but a theological formulation developed gradually within Catholicism in later centuries. The truth of “the Son of God”, by contrast, is a consistent, clear and fixed core revelation running from the Old Testament into the New Testament. ^PFP-00018

Surah 112 of the Qur’an rejects divine begetting in a physical sense, stating plainly that the True God neither begets nor is begotten. The definitive declaration in Qur’an 17:111 goes further, repudiating every form of divine sonship — including figurative, covenantal, and commissioned‑authority sonship — and is not limited merely to biological generation. ^PFP-00019

In contrast, multiple Old Testament passages prefigure and testify to the Messiah’s status as the Son of God. Isaiah 9:6¹ foretells that the Messiah will be the Wonderful Counsellor and Prince of Peace belonging to the Mighty One. The plural verb form in Genesis 1:26 hints at the Father and the Son participating jointly in creation. Psalm 2:7 formally establishes the Messiah as the Son of God. The Davidic Covenant in 2 Samuel chapter 7 also confirms the anointed Messiah’s sacred status as one subordinate to the Father, exercising authority delegated by Him. ^PFP-00020

The Messiah, the Son, is of one substance with the Father yet not identical to Him. He is the divinely sent Agent, commissioned by the Father and working alongside the Father. This inherent Father‑Son order in Scripture is the core doctrinal point wholly denied by the Qur’an and thoroughly disrupted by the Catholic doctrine of the Trinity. ^PFP-00021

## II. The Historical Foundation of Prophecy: The Fall of the Arian Powers and the Rise of the Papal Little Horn

Daniel chapter 7 prophesies that after Rome divides into ten horns, three Arian kingdoms will be uprooted, removing the secular powers that stood in the way of papal ascendancy. ^PFP-00022

Arian theology upheld the subordination of the Son: the Son was sent by the Father, inferior in essence, and not co‑equal with the Father. Catholic trinitarian theology teaches that the Father, Son and Holy Spirit are co‑equal in essence, with no order of subordination. ^PFP-00023

Centuries of doctrinal conflict ended with the total collapse of the Arian kingdoms, clearing the historical stage for the theopolitical power of the Catholic papal little horn to rule unrivalled. ^PFP-00024

## III. Final Distinction between the Twin 1260‑Year Prophetic Cycles

Scripture contains two prophetic cycles both lasting 1260 years, distinct in definition, divergent in starting points, and non‑conflicting. Though equal in duration, they begin at different times and therefore conclude in different years; they do not terminate in the same year. ^PFP-00025

Daniel’s 1260 years: the cycle of pure persecution of the saints Starting point: the ascendancy of the Holy Roman Empire, when Catholic doctrine gained full dominance across Western Europe and systematic, empire‑wide persecution of saints holding to the truth commenced. Defining marker: marked by the suffering of the saints. ^PFP-00026

Revelation’s 1260 years (forty‑two months): the cycle of the beast’s lawful reign and blasphemy against divine authority Fixed starting point: 538 CE Two historic events converged to fulfil this prophecy: ① Belisarius conquered the last remaining Arian kingdom, freeing papal authority from all secular checks and balances; ② The Third Council of Orléans issued an official decree abolishing the seventh‑day Sabbath of Scripture and imposing universal observance of Sunday, altering God’s commandments by formal religious law. Defining marker: marked by the beast usurping divine authority and exercising lawful dominion over the world. ^PFP-00027

The capture of the Pope by Napoleon in 1798, which inflicted a mortal wound upon the Papal States, constitutes a landmark event within Revelation’s cycle, yet it is not the common terminus of both cycles. The two cycles begin independently, run their separate 1260‑year courses, and end at different times. The final loss of the Papal States’ secular territorial sovereignty in 1870 marks a subsequent critical turning point. ^PFP-00028

## IV. Revelation Chapter 12: The Prophecy of the Woman’s Two Flights into the Wilderness

Revelation chapter 12 records two flights of the woman into the wilderness. These are not repetitions of one vision, but two successive historical stages. The woman symbolises Israel. Joseph’s dream in the Old Testament prefigures this symbol, and all features of the passage consistently point to the nation of Israel. ^PFP-00029

Rev. 12:6: The woman flees to a place prepared for her by God in the wilderness, where she is nourished for one thousand two hundred and sixty days. This passage follows the prophetic principle “a day for a year”. The period commences in 688 CE and extends until 1948 CE. ^PFP-00030

Many expositors mistakenly identify the Roman destruction of the Second Temple in 70 CE as the starting point of the woman’s flight. This is a misreading of the historical timeline. After the temple fell in 70 CE, the Jewish people were not fully expelled, nor was all indigenous worship severed. Rome did not empty Jerusalem of its Jewish population; successive later regimes permitted Jews to return to Jerusalem to dwell, to make pilgrimage, and to worship on holy ground. Jewish communities retained roots in the land of Palestine and had not entered a state of complete wilderness exile. ^PFP-00031

The historical turning point that definitively ended Jewish settlement and worship in the homeland and inaugurated full wilderness exile came in 688 CE. The Umayyad caliph Abd al‑Malik began construction of the Dome of the Rock on Jerusalem’s Temple Mount. The project was designed to occupy the sacred temple site permanently and supplant the foundation of Jewish faith. To complete the sanctification and Islamisation of the site, authorities expelled the remaining Jewish inhabitants of Jerusalem in large numbers and cleared the Temple Mount and the old city core. From that time onward, the Jewish population within Jerusalem dwindled almost to nothing. The Jewish people lost the right to dwell on the holy site, forfeited the lawful foundation for worship in their homeland, and possessed no indigenous place of faith. They entered fully into exile among the nations, cast by God into the wilderness. ^PFP-00032

It was this expulsion and replacement of the holy site in 688 that launched the 1260‑year period of wilderness protection for the woman who represents Israel: from 688 until the restoration of Israel in 1948, the children of Israel departed the holy land and scattered to dwell among the nations of Europe and the Americas, finding refuge in the wilderness of the nations. This precisely fulfils the one thousand two hundred and sixty days of Rev. 12:6, interpreted with the day‑for‑a‑year principle. ^PFP-00033

The war in heaven (Rev. 12:7–12) takes place after this period, when the great dragon is cast down to the earth. Once cast down, the dragon pursues the woman who bore the male child (12:13). ^PFP-00034

Rev. 12:14: The woman is given the two wings of the great eagle, to fly into the wilderness, to her own place, away from the serpent’s assault. There she is nourished for “a time, and times, and half a time” — equivalent to one thousand two hundred and sixty days. This second 1260‑day span is literal calendar days, not calculated by the day‑for‑a‑year rule. ^PFP-00035

The phrase “her own place” identifies the destination of this second flight as the wilderness lands of Europe and America where she had previously sojourned. This event occurs after the restoration of Israel in 1948. The two wings of the eagle may symbolise modern air transport, enabling the people of Israel to flee rapidly from end‑times persecution and return once more to the refuge lands of Europe and America. ^PFP-00036

This pattern follows the same typological principle as Nebuchadnezzar’s “seven times”. The seven times that fell upon Nebuchadnezzar were literal seven years for him personally; yet that same “seven times” also typifies a longer cycle calculated day‑for‑a‑year, amounting to 2520 prophetic years. One prophetic sign may therefore carry two layers of fulfilment: a short‑term literal fulfilment, and a long‑term fulfilment on the day‑for‑a‑year scale. ^PFP-00037

The narrative sequence of the text: First flight (verse 6): 688–1948, day for a year. Israel flees into the wilderness of Europe and America after the full Islamisation of the Temple Mount and the mass expulsion of the Jewish people. The war in heaven occurs, and the great dragon is cast down to the earth. The dragon unleashes persecution in the end times. The woman receives the eagle’s wings (aircraft) and flees once more to the same wilderness region (Europe and America), “her own place”, entering the second protected period of literal 1260 days. ^PFP-00038

Many exegetes argue that verses 6 and 14 are merely restatements of one single event, a common cyclical literary technique in apocalyptic writing. Nevertheless, the chronological markers, the manner of deliverance, the different calculation rules for the two 1260‑day spans, and the pivotal phrase “to her own place” do not support the view that this describes one single period. ^PFP-00039

In the first instance, God prepares a place beforehand, and the woman enters the wilderness. In the second, after the dragon is cast down and persecution arises anew, God grants the eagle’s wings for her to return to that same wilderness refuge. Though both segments share the same numerical length, they are calculated differently: one is a prophetic year‑day span, the other literal days. These are two separate protective cycles ordained by God for Israel, not one single historical period. ^PFP-00040

## V. Revelation Chapter 17: The Definitive Exposition of the Seven Heads and Ten Horns

### (A) Foundational Principles

The beast of Revelation 17 is the fourth beast of Daniel chapter 7: the Roman system. Scripture draws a clear distinction between the seven heads and the ten horns; they must never be confused. The seven heads: seven successive royal forms of the same Roman beast, rising and falling in chronological sequence, forming the linear primary hegemonic line. The ten horns: ten authorities coexisting side by side in the end times, developing in parallel. They do not succeed or replace the primary line of the seven heads, but represent contemporaneous national powers. ^PFP-00041

The seven heads constitute the successive historical main line of dominion; the ten horns are parallel coexistent powers of the end times. Their roles, symbolism, timelines and prophetic missions are entirely distinct and must not be conflated. ^PFP-00042

### (B) Fixed Chronology of the Seven Heads

First head: the unified Roman Empire (27 BCE – 395 CE) The undivided original form of the beast, the starting point of the whole Roman succession line. ^PFP-00043

Second head: the Eastern Roman Empire (Byzantium, 395–1453 CE) The longest‑lived, legitimate successor state of Rome after the imperial division. ^PFP-00044

Third head: the Western Roman Empire (395–476 CE) It coexisted with the East for a time but fell earlier. Within this symbolic prophetic framework, it stands as a distinct head, representing a separate phase after the beast’s division. Note: Although East and West Rome overlapped chronologically, within this prophetic symbolism they are treated as two separate heads and two distinct phases. The division of the empire itself is not counted as an additional head. ^PFP-00045

Fourth head: the Holy Roman Empire (800–1806 CE) Carrying forward the Western Roman succession, crowned by the Pope and dominated by the House of Habsburg. It formed the secular pillar of medieval Catholic authority and the pivotal stage when persecution of the saints reached its peak. ^PFP-00046

Fifth head: the Papal States (538–1798 CE; final loss of secular territory in 1870 CE) This is the little horn power of Daniel chapter 7, corresponding to the full forty‑two‑month period of the beast’s unhindered authority in Revelation. It marks the apex of papal secular power and the most important theopolitical authority among the seven heads. The capture of the Pope by Napoleon in 1798 inflicted a mortal wound upon the Papal States; 1870 represents the subsequent loss of Rome and most remaining secular territory. ^PFP-00047

Sixth head: the Austro‑Hungarian Empire (1867–1918 CE) The final legitimate continuation of the Habsburg main line, succeeding the Holy Roman Empire and upholding the Catholic Roman succession on continental Europe. It was the last major protective power of the papal system on the continent. ^PFP-00048

Seventh head: the Italian Fascist regime under Mussolini (1922–1943 CE) It fulfils the verse perfectly: “when he comes, he must continue a short space”. It endured for only a brief span and is the final and shortest of the seven heads. ^PFP-00049

Addendum: the eighth beast (Rev. 17:11) The beast itself is the eighth, ascending out of the seven heads and going into perdition. This corresponds to the restoration of Vatican sovereignty after the Lateran Treaty of 1929: a revived power arising from the healed mortal wound of the fifth head. It is not a new head, yet it is the final eschatological beast system. ^PFP-00050

### (C) Candidates Excluded from the Seven Heads

Under the criterion of this interpretation, which follows the Catholic Roman succession line, the following regimes are excluded from the seven heads: ^PFP-00051

- The Ottoman Empire: rooted in Islamic tradition, it lies outside the Catholic Roman succession line and is treated as a separate opposing theological power.
- Napoleon’s French Empire: born out of a secular revolutionary movement hostile to the papacy, extremely short‑lived, and not part of this continuous line of Roman authority.
- The Spanish Empire: a maritime colonial hegemony, counted among the nations of the ten horns, not a successive head of the main line.

^PFP-00052

### (D) The Precise Time Window of the Vision (Snapshot: 1870–1918 CE)

The vision John saw is a snapshot frozen within the years 1870–1918 CE. Only within this window are all prophetic conditions fulfilled simultaneously: ^PFP-00053

- The fifth head, the Papal States, had already received its mortal wound in 1798 and lost its secular territory fully in 1870; the wound remained unhealed prior to the Lateran Treaty of 1929.
- The sixth head, the Austro‑Hungarian Empire, was reigning — “the one that is”.
- The seventh head, Italian Fascism, had not yet arisen.
- The entities that would later constitute the ten horns were established in God’s prophetic plan but had not yet received independent national sovereignty.

^PFP-00054

### (E) Definition and Criteria for the Ten Horns

#### Criteria for the ten horns

- Core territories lie within the provinces of the ancient Roman Empire
- Powers arising after the fall of Rome
- Coexisting parallel authorities, not successive hegemonies of the main line
- In the end times, they receive authority alongside the beast and ultimately destroy Babylon the Great, the harlot

^PFP-00055

### (F) Exegesis of Greek Tenses in Revelation 17:12

Verse text: “The ten horns which you saw are ten kings, which have received no kingdom as yet; but receive power as kings one hour with the beast.” Two Greek verb tenses form the central key to the prophetic timeline of the whole passage: οὔπω ἔλαβον βασιλείαν (they have not yet received a kingdom): aorist tense. From the viewpoint of John’s vision snapshot, at that moment the ten kings had not yet obtained sovereign kingdoms. λαμβάνουσιν (they receive authority): present tense, which in apocalyptic grammar may refer to a future event already ordained by God and certain to come to pass. Rev. 17:13 uses future‑tense verbs: they shall give their power unto the beast. ^PFP-00056

### (G) Textual Examination of Revelation 13:1 and 1:10 (Correction based on the HRV Hebrew Roots Version)

This exposition consistently draws upon the HRV (Hebrew Roots Version), grounded in Hebrew and Aramaic source‑text research. The core conclusions are as follows: ^PFP-00057

Background of textual variants Revelation 13:1 exhibits manuscript divergence. The dominant Greek manuscripts read plural “many names of blasphemy”. The KJV and HRV preserve the singular “the name of blasphemy”, which represents the original reading. ^PFP-00058

Core value of the HRV The HRV translates from the Hebrew and Aramaic original linguistic background rather than following later dominant Greek manuscripts, bringing it closer to the original context in which Revelation was composed. ^PFP-00059

Textual correction for Rev. 1:10 Mainstream Greek versions render the phrase “the Lord’s day”. This Greek phrase occurs nowhere else in the New Testament and is a later added term. The HRV, together with ancient Ethiopic manuscripts, preserves the primitive reading: “the first day of the week”. This demonstrates that “the Lord’s day” is an alteration introduced by later ecclesiastical editors and does not appear in the original text. ^PFP-00060

Final doctrinal conclusion The single blasphemous name upon the beast’s heads is the one continuous core blasphemous doctrine running through every stage of the seven‑headed Roman beast: the Catholic doctrine of the Trinity. ^PFP-00061

### (H) The Eschatological Outcome of the Harlot and the Ten Horns

Babylon the Great, the harlot riding upon the beast in Revelation 17, symbolises the Catholic religious system and its spiritual doctrinal authority. The harlot rides upon the sea beast and operates through that secular political system; the harlot herself is not identical with secular state power. ^PFP-00062

The ten horns described in the text grow out of the beast. They are powers internal to the sea‑beast system, not external allies making a pact with the beast. The ten horns are secular European powers that arose within the bounds of the Roman Empire after its division. ^PFP-00063

Prophecy foretells the end‑times development: the ten horns upon the beast will finally hate the harlot who rides it, make her desolate, eat her flesh, and burn her with fire. In other words, these secular European powers belonging to the beast system will judge and destroy the Catholic religious system. ^PFP-00064

### (I) Defining the Destruction of the Fourth Beast

Daniel chapter 7 prophesies that the fourth beast will be broken in pieces and destroyed. The judgment that falls is upon the governmental and doctrinal system that opposes the Most High and blasphemes the truth. It does not mean the total annihilation of the European peoples or geographical nations. ^PFP-00065

The political entities represented by the ten horns may keep changing, reorganising and evolving. Only the beast power structure inherited from Rome, which persists in blaspheming the truth and opposing God, is the object of God’s judgment and total destruction. ^PFP-00066

### (J) The Final Consummation of the Kingdom

When the small horn of the fourth beast in the book of Daniel (which is identical to the resurrected eighth beast in the book of Revelation) perishes under judgment, the final promise of Daniel chapter 7 is fulfilled: the kingdom, dominion, and the glory of all kingdoms under heaven shall be given to the saints of the Most High. ^PFP-00067

This is the same eschatological scene as the millennial kingdom described in Revelation. Within the millennial kingdom ruled by the Messiah: ^PFP-00068

- The Messiah reigns as King over all the earth;
- Jerusalem is the centre of governance for the kingdom;
- The nations and peoples of the earth continue to exist and are not exterminated;
- Every beast system hostile to God is permanently abolished;
- The Messiah executes God’s laws and statutes across the whole earth, guiding all nations and peoples to walk in the truth of Yehowah.

^PFP-00069

From that time onward, no blasphemous beast system remains on the earth, no power stands opposed to the truth. The whole earth is ruled by the Messiah as King, with the law of God as its highest standard, fulfilling God’s original purpose in creation and redemption. ^PFP-00070

### (K) Re-Examining the Core Cornerstones of Christian Doctrine: Redemption of the Cross, Justification by Faith, and the Messiah’s Fulfillment of the Law

In the last three chapters, we re-examine three cornerstones of Christian doctrine: the redemption of the cross, justification by faith, and the Messiah’s fulfilment of the Law. According to the Passover pattern, the Messiah’s work on the cross marks the starting point of redemption: He bore the penalty for sin, provided forgiveness, and gave the Holy Spirit so that believers may receive power to keep the Law. This work does not remove the lifelong calling to obey and keep the commandments; hence the teaching of “once saved, always saved” conflicts with the unified witness of Scripture. The widespread framework of justification by faith rests on interpretive shifts introduced by the Septuagint, isolating a one‑time act of belief as the sole ground of justification and severing faith from works. Grammatical examination of the Hebrew original, the Epistle of James and the First Epistle of John demonstrates that the righteousness described in Scripture consists of abiding covenant faithfulness. The righteous, though prone to occasional weakness, obtain forgiveness through repentance and mercy, and genuine faith inevitably yields obedient conduct. When the Messiah declared that He came to fulfil the Law, He did not mean that He would keep the commandments on behalf of believers to release them from obligation. Instead, He elevated the standard of the Law to the motives of the heart, magnifying and honouring it. Grace is not a licence to live apart from the Law, but the gift that empowers sincere obedience to its commandments. ^PFP-00071
