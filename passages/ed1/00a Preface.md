# Preface

This volume is a sequel to Perfectly Fulfilled Prophecy, the Destiny of Mankind. In the earlier work, I established a rigorous biblical chronology framework for examining biblical numerical prophecies, demonstrating that such prophecies allow only one definite way of fulfillment, with a chronological margin of error limited to approximately six months. While that book examined most numerical prophecies of the Old Testament and a few from the New Testament, it did not fully unpack the chronological sequence and textual details of the Book of Revelation. The present volume therefore fills this gap. Centered on Revelation, it draws together the prophecies of Daniel, Isaiah, Zechariah, Ezekiel and other prophetic books, to reconstruct a consistent and panoramic eschatological timeline within one unified prophetic system. ^PFP-00001

This book maintains that Revelation is not an independent, brand-new revelation, but the consummation of the prophetic witness begun in the Old Testament prophets. Departing from the fragmented interpretive approaches common in many contemporary commentaries, it weaves the seals, trumpets, bowls, the beast system, the mark of the beast, and the battle of Armageddon into one continuous coherent timeline. For textual grounding, this study prioritizes ancient versions closer to the original manuscripts, including the Hebrew Roots Version (HRV) and the Lamsa Aramaic Bible. It identifies later additions and alterations introduced in subsequent copies and ecclesiastical translations, and builds its interpretation upon the internal consistency of Scripture itself, to recover the original, unadulterated biblical eschatological framework. ^PFP-00002

This work systematically resolves several long-debated prophetic puzzles. It carefully distinguishes two separate 1260‑day prophetic cycles in Scripture, which share the same length yet differ in starting point, definition, reckoning method and historical scope. It expounds the two wilderness flights of the woman in Revelation 12: the first is a long historical cycle interpreted on the principle “a day for a year”; the second is a short refuge period reckoned according to literal days in the end time. It also gives a full exegesis of the seven heads and ten horns of the Roman beast in Revelation 13 and 17: the seven heads represent successive hegemonic powers along one linear historical lineage, while the ten horns are contemporaneous authorities coexisting in the last days. The Greek verb tenses, ancient textual variants and precise historical markers together fix the unique time window for the vision John beheld. ^PFP-00003

This volume also lays its foundational doctrinal boundary: the doctrine of the Trinity is a theological construct developed later within Catholic tradition, not the original revelation of Scripture. The consistent biblical witness is that the Most High Father is the sole Source; the Messiah Son is sent, appointed and commissioned by the Father, acting as His subordinate divine Agent. This Father‑Son order upheld in Scripture was defended by the Arians, suppressed by the little horn papal power. The fall of the three Arian kingdoms and the rise of papal authority constitute the historical fulfillment of Daniel’s prophecy. ^PFP-00004

Within this unified chronological framework, most visions, time periods and beast‑related institutions in Revelation have already been fulfilled in history. Only the final outpouring of the trumpets and bowls, the coming of the Messiah, and the establishment of the millennial kingdom remain future events. From the Old Testament prophets to the final visions of Revelation, the whole corpus of biblical prophecy forms one complete, harmonious plan of judgment and redemption. ^PFP-00005

The writing of this book arises from personal verification and a burden borne from years of prophetic research. My earlier book Perfectly Fulfilled Prophecy, the Destiny of Mankind was published in 2019. Through careful chronological computation of biblical numerical prophecies, I identified two major eschatological markers: first, a major war would break out in Israel after September 15, 2023; second, the Messiah would come around May 2027. ^PFP-00006

What followed has been striking confirmation. The prophecy I published years in advance was fulfilled literally: on October 7, 2023, Israel entered its most severe and far‑reaching conflict in decades. The warfare has continued to this day, falling squarely within the prophetic window I set forth after September 15, 2023. This attests to the verifiability and precision of biblical numerical prophecy. ^PFP-00007

Moreover, the timeline for Messiah’s coming in 2027 has received independent corroboration in recent years. Other researchers have arrived at the same year by separate lines of prophetic inquiry. Even two Orthodox Jewish rabbis have publicly noted that 2027 may bring momentous events connected with the manifestation of the Messiah. Unrelated lines of study converge on the same appointed season marked out by God. ^PFP-00008

At an earlier stage of my research, however, my understanding of Revelation’s fulfillment timeline remained incomplete. I once assumed that most prophecies in Revelation still awaited fulfillment in the future. This created a critical tension: if Revelation’s prophecies were yet unfulfilled, the chronology pointing to the Messiah’s coming in 2027 would seem unstable, and I questioned my own long‑standing calculations. ^PFP-00009

This uncertainty drove me into sustained, careful examination: verse‑by‑verse textual study, historical cross‑reference, comparison among ancient versions, and reconstruction of the visionary sequence. The conclusion reached is this: the vast majority of the prophecies in Revelation have already been historically fulfilled. Only the final judgment, the outpouring of the trumpets and bowls, the return of the Messiah and the establishment of His kingdom lie ahead. ^PFP-00010

With this realization, the chronological calculations from my 2019 publication, the fulfilled Israel war prophecy of 2023, the converging testimonies concerning 2027, and the full Revelation timeline established in this volume fit seamlessly together, confirming one another. The whole biblical eschatological witness becomes one non‑contradictory, consummated system. ^PFP-00011

The purpose of this book is to lay out this verified prophetic thread, running from the Old Testament through history and onward to the end. It demonstrates that God’s prophecies are faultless, unbroken and true, until the Messiah returns, establishes His millennial kingdom of everlasting justice and peace, and completes the redemptive purpose for which the world was created. ^PFP-00012
