# Goals

Turn *Perfectly Fulfilled Prophecies in the Book of Revelation* (by Josh's dad; 100+ page .docx) into:

- **Short videos**, each introducing one idea from the book, one at a time.
- **An interactive website** where readers explore how the ideas connect, through a mind map and explicit "if P, then Q" relationships. Readers can trace each claim back to the book's exact text, examine its scriptural and historical support, and see what evidence or counterarguments could strengthen, weaken or overturn it.

## Stages
1. **Organize the text**: individually referenceable passages with stable IDs, then claims, arguments and objections. ← current
2. Website (Quartz vs. custom, decided after the pilot).
3. Videos, mapped onto claims.

See [[Success test]] for how we judge the pilot.
