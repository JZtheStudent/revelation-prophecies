# Content notes

Observations about the **content** noticed during conversion: repetition across chapters, or places where the text may disagree with itself. These are **not** formatting problems and **nothing has been changed**. The passages stay verbatim. They're noted for (a) the author's review and (b) v1, when claims and arguments are extracted.

## Repeated material
- **Seven heads / ten horns framework:** Introduction § V (PFP-00041 onward) and Chapter 4 § I (PFP-00151 onward) cover the same ground (the head list, excluded regimes, the 1870–1918 window, Rev 17:12 grammar, the singular blasphemous name) in different wording. In v1, one claim may cite both passages.
- **Revelation 13:1 singular name:** discussed in Introduction (G), Chapter 1 § I(2) and Chapter 4 § I.8.

## Possible internal differences (for the author to confirm)
- **When the Fifth Head received its wound:** Introduction PFP-00047 and Chapter 4 PFP-00162 date the deadly wound to 1798 and the final loss of territory to 1870. Chapter 4 PFP-00171 says the Papal States "lost secular territory and received its wound in 1870". Possibly just compressed wording.
- **Ten-horn criteria:** Introduction PFP-00055 lists four criteria; Chapter 4 PFP-00173 lists five, adding "Catholicism as the dominant faith".
- **Chapter reference:** Chapter 5's opening note (PFP-00204) says it follows the framework "established in Chapter 5". It probably means Chapter 4, which sets out the seven heads and ten horns.
- **Bible translation varies:** Chapter 3 quotes Revelation in modern English ("Come!"), while Chapter 5 (PFP-00215, PFP-00217) quotes it in KJV wording ("thou sawest", "whore"). This may be deliberate. Note it for the scripture callouts' translation labels.
- **Revelation 16:15 wording:** Chapter 1 (PFP-00082) argues that the HRV's "Behold, **He** comes as a thief" is the original reading, against the mainstream "I come". Chapter 8's quotation of Rev 16 (PFP-00285) uses the mainstream "Behold, **I** come like a thief". The author may want the quotation to match the reading he argues for.
- **Tropical year value:** Chapter 8 (PFP-00287) uses "the tropical year of 365.20 days". The usual value is about 365.24 days. It doesn't change the results: 1411 days ≈ 3.86 years and 2554 days ≈ 6.99 years either way. The arithmetic (5 × 29.5 + 1260 + 3.5 = 1411; 1411 + 1143 = 2554) checks out.
- **Cross-references to other chapters:** Chapter 10 says "as explained in the preceding chapter, redemption at the cross marks the starting point of salvation" (PFP-00304) and refers to "the Passover typology and two‑tier redemption structure set out in earlier chapters" (PFP-00313). Chapter 9 (the preceding chapter) is about the first and second death, and the Introduction (PFP-00071) says the cross / justification / Law material comes in "the last three chapters". Either the chapter order changed during writing, or these references point to chapters still to come. Chapter 11 opens with "Recap of Previous Chapters" (PFP-00315), saying "we have confirmed" that Zechariah and Revelation share a two-stage style, using Zechariah 9:9–10 as the example. No chapter so far discusses Zechariah 9. Chapter 13's opening note (PFP-00348) says it "follows the two‑layer structure of redemption from the previous chapter", but Chapter 12 is about the millennial order (circumcision, the law, the millennial temple). Chapter 13 itself is the "justification by faith" chapter the Introduction places among "the last three chapters". Re-check once all chapters are in. (Chapter 9's reference to the books-of-life framework "in Chapter 10" does match.)
- **Version name:** "Hebrew Roots Version" (most places) vs. "Hebrew Root Version" (Chapter 4 § I.8 heading).
