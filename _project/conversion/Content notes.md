# Content notes

Observations about the **content** noticed during conversion: repetition across chapters, or places where the text may disagree with itself. These are **not** formatting problems and **nothing has been changed**. The passages stay verbatim. They're noted for (a) the author's review and (b) v1, when claims and arguments are extracted.

## Repeated material
- **Seven heads / ten horns framework:** Introduction § V (PFP-00041 onward) and Chapter 4 § I (PFP-00151 onward) cover the same ground (the head list, excluded regimes, the 1870–1918 window, Rev 17:12 grammar, the singular blasphemous name) in different wording. In v1, one claim may cite both passages.
- **Revelation 13:1 singular name:** discussed in Introduction (G), Chapter 1 § I(2) and Chapter 4 § I.8.

## Possible internal differences (for the author to confirm)
- **When the Fifth Head received its wound:** Introduction PFP-00047 and Chapter 4 PFP-00162 date the deadly wound to 1798 and the final loss of territory to 1870. Chapter 4 PFP-00171 says the Papal States "lost secular territory and received its wound in 1870". Possibly just compressed wording.
- **Ten-horn criteria:** Introduction PFP-00055 lists four criteria; Chapter 4 PFP-00173 lists five, adding "Catholicism as the dominant faith".
- **Version name:** "Hebrew Roots Version" (most places) vs. "Hebrew Root Version" (Chapter 4 § I.8 heading).
